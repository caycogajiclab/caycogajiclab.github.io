N. Alex Cayco Gajic is a Full Professor at the École Normale Supérieure in Paris, and obtained a PhD in Applied Mathematics from the University of Washington.

The lab works at the intersection of machine learning, mathematical modelling, and neuroscience. Our long-term research goal is to develop a mathematical framework to understand the geometry and dynamics of learning in artificial neural networks and the brain.

At ENS, Alex leads the Neural Representations of Learning and Behavior team in the Group for Neural Theory, with secondary affiliations in the ENS Quantitative Biology Centre and the Paris Artificial Intelligence Research Institute (PRAIRIE).
