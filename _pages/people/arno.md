### Arno Granier

Before joining the Cayco-Gajic lab at Ecole Normale Supérieure, I studied computational neuroscience at University of Bern (with Walter Senn) and the Institute of Neuroinformatics Zürich (with Matthew Cook). My research now focuses on (i) continual learning, context-dependent processing, and structured contextual representations in recurrent neural networks and (ii) gradient-free optimization on compact parametric encodings of connectivity in neural networks.
