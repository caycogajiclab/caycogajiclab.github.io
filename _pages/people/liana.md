### Liana Akobian

I am a first-year PhD student funded by an ED Sciences Cognitives fellowship. During my MSc in Data Science at the Technical University of Vienna, I worked with [Dr. Manuel Zimmer](https://neurodevbio.univie.ac.at/zimmer-research/) to model C. elegans neural dynamics using state-switching linear dynamical systems and control theory. Prior to my master's I obtained my BSc in Computer Science from the University of Vienna. In Alex’s group, I use data-driven models to study non-normal dynamics as an alternative mechanism to attractors for short-term memory and sequential computation.
